Student Onboarding

Use the first week to help students open a recording, change the mix, and pin a note that names something they heard.

Before Day One

Send the course invitation from the Lab portal and keep the invitation window open through add/drop. Prepare a Week 1 folder with two or three recordings you have already split and checked. Choose Class: listen and add notes and press Save sharing so current class members can complete the first annotation task. Post links to the app and this documentation on your course site.

Day One

Allow about fifteen minutes for students to sign in together. They can open the app from My classes and use CUNY Login. Leave time for multi-factor prompts, then direct everyone to the Week 1 folder under Class Folders. Load all opens its recordings in the rack.

Ask everyone to solo the bass on the same song for thirty seconds. Clear the solo, then mute the bass and replay the passage. Invite students to describe what changed and pin one note at a moment that supports their answer. Check that each student can save a note before the class leaves.

First Week

Assign One Stem at a Time on a recording from the Week 1 folder. Students who want to practice importing audio can also split a song under Personal. That optional task uses one split run and keeps the new recording outside the course.

Introduce Listening Guy later in the week through Ask, Then Verify. Explain that the model cannot hear the audio. Students should use the mixer to check a claim from its guide before they ask a follow-up question.

Explain the Course Policy

Before students assign work to the course, explain that the acknowledgment lets you review their new course splits and Listening Guy conversations. Classmates gain access when an instructor shares a course folder. Choose the folder permission and press Save sharing to allow listening or note-taking. Existing personal splits stay private to the student's account.

Demonstrate the Share with selector and let students find Personal themselves. The choice gives them a place to practice before they submit new work to the course.

Common First-Week Problems

If a student cannot see the course, check the roster in Class Workspace. The student may still need to accept an invitation, or the invitation window may have closed. If the recording is silent, ask the student to press play once. In a live session, Join shared audio gives the browser the click it needs to allow playback.

If a split fails, check the file's format and its 100 MB limit, then try again or use an Archive search. The provider may also be busy. A failed split releases its place in the daily allowance.

If Listening Guy cannot answer, check the remaining turns and the panel's message. The provider may be temporarily unavailable, and the mixer and notes remain available. For other problems, students can use Report a bug in the footer. Students can use CUNY Login or choose No account? and provide their first name, last name, and a .edu email address. The form adds page and release information without automatically attaching audio, notes, or conversations.
