Classes

The Classes page holds one class at a time: its shared folders and, for instructors, your own class splits and its students, including the Listening Guy conversations attached to course work.

Assigning Work to a Course

On the Splitter tab, choose a course under Share with. Opening the Splitter directly starts at Personal, while New class split on the Classes page selects that class. Before assigning new work to a course, check the acknowledgment that your instructor can review those splits and their Listening Guy conversations. Classmates gain access when an instructor shares a course folder with them.

After you check the acknowledgment, each new split belongs to the selected course. Changing the course clears the checkbox. If class access changes or checking access fails, choose a workspace before creating a split. Choosing Personal clears the class from the link so a reload respects your choice. Existing personal work stays personal.

The Classes Page

Use the Class selector to switch between courses you belong to or teach. Instructors see three sections that stay in place: Folders, My splits, and Students. Students see the folders shared with their class. New class split opens the Splitter with that class selected under Share with. Check its acknowledgment to create new course work. Instructors can open Class settings to change course options, such as shared stem labels.

The page updates while you keep it open. Refresh reloads it when you need to check a recent change.

Roster and Usage

Under Students, instructors see every enrolled student in the roster, including students who have no splits. Each row shows the student's assigned splits, statuses, dates, and a link to read their Listening Guy conversation. Counts cover the roster entries currently loaded, so load further pages before treating a count as the course total. An incomplete or missing activity record is not proof that a student has never used the app. Recorded operations and browser reports have different coverage; use the dates and explanation shown with the totals.

The Lab's admission records determine enrollment. The app checks membership on every protected request. A student who withdraws loses course access, and a copied folder link cannot grant access on its own.

Folders and Sharing

Instructors create folders under Folders. A folder opens under its own row. Use Add splits there to add your class splits, or open a student under Students to add one of theirs. On the Splitter, Add to class folder in a class split's menu does the same. A folder starts with Instructors only access and accepts only splits assigned to its course. Remove takes a split out of the folder. Delete folder removes the folder and leaves its splits in the class.

An instructor chooses a folder permission and presses Save sharing. Instructors only keeps the folder available to the course's instructors. Class: listen and read notes lets current class members play the stems and read existing notes. Choose Class: listen and add notes when students need to annotate a shared recording.

Share the folder link, or direct students to Folders on the Classes page. Each viewer must currently belong to the course. The folder exposes only the splits inside it; original uploads and Listening Guy conversations stay outside the shared view. Revoking sharing hides the folder at once. An expired split remains as a placeholder, and the folder does not extend its retention. My folders on the Splitter tab are separate: they are private to you and belong to no class.

Reviewing Conversations

Instructors can open a student's course split and Listening Guy sessions from the roster. The review view lets you read the conversation without generating an answer or spending a turn. It also prevents edits and forwarding. Personal splits and conversations from before the student acknowledged the course policy stay private.
