## What the saved evaluations establish

The historical [CLAP candidate audit](2026-08-09-instrument-discovery-candidate.md) reports 42 expected groups. Those expectations are **not fact-verified exact-track/excerpt ground truth**. Likewise, provisional negatives in the [YAMNet comparator audit](2026-08-09-yamnet-comparator-gate.md) cannot be counted as factual instrument absences. The documentary checks above establish no exhaustive negative inventory for any of the twelve recordings. Unknown labels must not become factual false positives or false negatives.

Preserve the historical, hash-bound reports and expectation manifests as they were; this audit qualifies their interpretation rather than retroactively changing their results. Raw scores and rankings remain useful diagnostics, but neither their thresholds nor genre-based expectations establish recognition accuracy.

Root's silent replay of the six frozen Tab output MP3s verified all six baseline hashes and ran the exact released `05d4abf0147f6d1acdff0db34b1543dc877f2665` composer. It invoked zero encoders, display writes, or provider calls. This confirms that the released guard rejects this bad control. It does not establish new instrument recognition or explain the artifact's cause.
